Intel GPU D270 is designed for the inference requirements that become important in those environments, including prefill processing, larger context, memory capacity, concurrency, and power efficiency.

Intel GPU D270 focuses on the needs of deployed AI applications, including prompt processing, high-concurrency serving, memory capacity, efficient power use, and system-level performance.
