Show HN -> a ton of automated email pitches

I posted Airdash on Show HN last week. Over the next four days, 17 services wrote to the Airdash inbox to sell me something, and nearly all of them were written by software that read my HN title and nothing else.

Let's go through it.

Seven of the 17 are launch boards and directories: AI tool lists, "Product of the Week" battles, a 3D city map of SaaS products. The hook is usually a dofollow backlink, the price somewhere between free and $49. The other ten sell security scans, competitor reports, agent testing, demo videos, Reddit promotion, newsletter sponsorships, distribution to other people's coding agents, and one wants an affiliate deal.

"SQLite databases for agents with an Airtable view" shows up in almost every email, reworded just enough to look like someone read it. One directory pasted my whole Show HN title in as the product name, so it would love to feature "I built SQLite databases for your agents with an Airtable view for you."

"No follow-ups, promise." "This is the only email you'll get about it." "Not a mailing list, just a one-time note." One of them then sent me the same pitch three times.

Here they are in order of arrival, Madrid time. I replaced the names of the services and the people with [brackets] and took out the links, to protect the innocent.
