The people you are trying to reach have more to look at than ever, and reaching them means showing up where they already look, in search, AI answers, social media, and in the feeds and communities they trust. Lovable now helps you grow your audience across all these channels, from the same chat where you build. In May, every app built with Lovable became readable and indexable by search engines and AI tools. Today, you can go a lot further. Ask Lovable how to grow your app, and it uses the full context of your app to create a growth plan and help you carry it out across organic content and SEO, social media campaigns on Facebook and Instagram or Google Ads campaigns across Search, YouTube, and Maps.

Here's how it works.

Set a goal, get a plan

Tell Lovable what you want, for example "My first 100 signups", "More bookings this month". It reviews what it already knows about your goal, app, target audience, and current traffic, and creates a plan with the actions to take to get there.

Sometimes that's launching an ad campaign. Often, especially for a brand-new app, it's foundational work: making sure your app can be discovered in Google or AI search, sharpening the offer on your landing page, sharing your app across social media, finding leads and talking to potential customers, or making sure your local business shows up on Google Maps. Lovable will suggest the next steps based on your own context, and you review and approve before acting on any recommendations.

You can change the goal at any time, and Lovable iterates with you.

Launch ads on Google, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram directly from Lovable

To start reaching and converting high-intent customers on Google Search, Maps, and YouTube, simply ask Lovable to connect your existing Google Ads account or create one. Lovable will install a Google Tag to measure the conversions that matter to your goal, and recommend the right campaign type.

Once your campaign is live, Google's built-in AI connects you with your ideal audience at the right time, whether they're searching on Google or watching their favorite videos on YouTube. If you're new to Google Ads you could qualify for Google Ads credits to jumpstart your first campaign.

To reach new users across Facebook and Instagram, sign in with Facebook Login for Business and Lovable connects to your ad account and Facebook Page. If you don't have them yet, you can create both in the same sign-in window. Lovable then adds the Meta Pixel to your app. The Pixel is a small piece of code that records when a visitor does the thing you care about, like signing up or buying, so you can see what your ads produce. If you don't have a Pixel, Lovable creates one for you. From there, Lovable builds image and video campaigns for Facebook and Instagram with a Sales, Leads, or Traffic objective.

After creating your campaign, review by Meta starts right away. Once your campaign is approved, you can launch it when you're ready.

You approve before anything spends

Before a campaign is created, Lovable summarizes the key inputs for your review: daily budget, target locations, languages, goals, and tracking. No ad campaigns will go live until you approve them.

Billing for your campaigns is set up directly within your Google Ads and Meta account. Lovable does not bill you directly or add a service fee to your ad spend. Creating and iterating on the growth plan and generating ad assets consumes regular Lovable credits.

Once a campaign is running, results show up in the Analytics tab, or you can ask Lovable for help analyzing performance in the chat. Ask it to pause, change the budget, reach a different audience, or try new copy, and Lovable applies the change. Ask for a dashboard, and it builds one. Lovable suggests what to change and makes the changes you ask for, but will not change a campaign on its own.

Be found in search with better SEO

Ranking in search takes three things working together: the fundamentals that let search engines and AI tools read your app, content people want to read, and links from places they already trust. Lovable now helps with all three.

It starts with the fundamentals: indexable pages, metadata and titles, sitemap, robots.txt, and connecting to Google Search Console. Lovable checks them in the SEO review and can fix what's off.

With Semrush built in, Lovable then finds what your app can realistically rank for, reviews how your existing pages perform, and is able to create the landing pages and content to rank for people, AI search, and agents.

The third part is new. Based on what you've built and who it's for, Lovable recommends where your product should show up beyond your own site: the forums, communities, directories, and social platforms where your audience already spends time, and where a mention can earn you a backlink as well as a visitor. Lovable drafts the posts and profiles for you. You publish them from your own accounts, so the voice and the relationships stay yours.

Creative assets made from your app

Lovable can create images, launch videos, animated product demos, video ads, landing pages, ad copy, and social media posts based on your product and brand identity. Before proposing anything, Lovable researches search interest for your topic and what similar businesses are running, and writes a short research report you can read. Then it proposes three creative directions. Pick one, adjust it, or upload your own images and videos instead.
