App Store 2026 in numbers: AI, pricing, abandoned apps

作者: Obelisk Club 来源: Obelisk Club 快照日期: 2026-11-06

Research · Data as of November 6, 2026

We looked at 178,637 apps that appeared in the App Store top charts of 173 countries and regions since October 9, 2026. Here are the answers to five questions.

Key findings

  1. About 1 in 10 apps in the charts mention AI in their store listing (9.8%); in Graphics & Design it is 43%.
  2. Apps that sell in-app purchases or subscriptions are 3 times as likely to mention AI as plain free apps (19.9% vs 6.6%).
  3. 26.7% of free-to-download apps sell in-app purchases or subscriptions; most in Graphics & Design (60.2%), fewest in Shopping (11.5%).
  4. 20.3% of apps in the charts have not been updated for a year or more; 9.8% for over three years.
  5. In 1-2 star reviews the most common complaint is subscription & price (12.7%), ahead of bugs & broken features (10.9%) and login & account (10%).

5. What bad reviews complain about

We read 165,735 US App Store reviews of 3,912 apps. Among 1-2 star reviews, 12.7% complain about subscription & price, 10.9% about bugs & broken features and 10% about login & account. One review can fall under several themes.

How to read these numbers

  • Scope: 178,637 iPhone, iPad and Mac apps that appeared in an App Store top chart since October 9, 2026. In 12 large markets that means the overall and category charts for free, paid and top grossing apps; elsewhere the overall free chart. Apps never seen in a chart are left out.
  • Categories: the app's primary App Store category, with names in other languages merged. Categories with fewer than 1,500 apps have no row of their own.
  • Mentions AI: the name or the first 1,500 characters of the description contain AI, GPT, artificial intelligence, machine learning, generative, LLM or chatbot. It shows the app markets itself with AI, not that its features depend on it.
  • Sells in-app purchases or subscriptions: free to download, and the description mentions a subscription, in-app purchase, premium, trial, unlock, Pro or a monthly or yearly price. Apps that say nothing may still sell something, so this share is on the low side.
  • Independent developer: at most 5 of the developer's apps seen in the charts, no app with 30,000 ratings or more, and a seller name that does not read as a company (Inc, Corp, GmbH and the like). We only see apps that charted, so this share may run high.
  • Last update: the release date of the current version on the US App Store.
  • Reviews: recent public reviews on the US App Store. Themes come from keywords (for example subscription, ads, crash); shares are of 1-2 star reviews, except feature requests, which are of all reviews. Recent reviews lean negative, so we do not publish the overall share of bad reviews.
  • Ratings: only apps with at least 50 ratings count.
